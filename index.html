What are the undergraduate admission requirements?

Applicants submit transcripts, a personal statement, and one recommendation. Test scores are optional for most programs. International students may need proof of English proficiency.

When is the application deadline?

Early decision is November 1. Regular decision is January 15. Transfer and rolling intakes vary by program—check the Admissions calendar for exact dates.

Is financial aid available for international students?

Yes. Merit scholarships and need-based aid are available to eligible international students. Complete the Kinfield Aid Profile with your application.

Can I visit campus before applying?

Absolutely. Book a guided tour online, join Open Campus Day, or schedule a virtual meeting with an admissions counselor.

How do I contact housing services?

Email housing@kinfield.edu or call (555) 010-2911. First-year students are guaranteed a residence hall placement when they apply by May 1.
